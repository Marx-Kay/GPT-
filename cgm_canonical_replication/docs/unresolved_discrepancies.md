# Remaining evidence-supported discrepancies

1. **Calibration documentation:** Table2 prints rounded cubic coefficients. They define the baseline; historical full precision is reported only as sensitivity. The income level shift and NCHS survival sequence are not fully recoverable from the published tables. Their provenance is recorded, but exact paper calibration cannot be certified.
2. **Initial condition:** W20=0 and nu20=0 are explicit choices, not values proven by a complete published initial-distribution statement. Registered sensitivities change initial permanent dispersion and starting wealth without changing the optimal kernel.
3. **Policy conditioning:** p497 says normalize nu to one, although multiplicative exp(nu)=1 corresponds to nu=0. Figure2 comparisons explicitly condition on nu=0; a graph match cannot settle the notational ambiguity.
4. **Timing/aggregation:** Eq9 identifies pre-income financial W, but p497 also loosely calls cash X wealth. All three W/X/saving series are exported. Figure3's large gap is not resolved by simply relabelling these quantities.
5. **Welfare rule convention:** Eq12's W and future wage definition require implementation choices in the discrete constrained model. These are explicit in welfare_method.md. Figure11's approximation path agrees with the literal Eq16 rule after correct extraction, so its remaining welfare gap is not attributed to a missing or mislabelled rule.
6. **Return support:** Eq6's unbounded Gaussian return assigns tiny positive probability to negative gross stock returns, in tension with almost-sure nonnegative wealth under Eq7–8. Appendix A's finite Gaussian quadrature avoids those nodes at the registered resolutions. Neither clipping nor changing return law is performed. Numerical acceptance applies to that approximation and positive realized simulation paths; it is not a proof that the exact unbounded-normal infinite-tail problem is well posed.
7. **Publication version:** All core numerical gates and independent checks must pass before this is called a validated numerical reconstruction. Even then, unmatched Figure2/3 and Table6 quantities do not establish whether an unavailable implementation/version detail differs. No parameter is fitted to remove the gap.

Stopping rule: once registered tests, refinements, input checks and disclosed-assumption sensitivities are complete, do not invent further parameter sweeps. The published numerical outputs are validation targets, not optimization objectives.
